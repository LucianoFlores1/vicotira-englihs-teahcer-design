# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (single page, vanilla JS only where needed). Chosen by the user for instant mobile load and free hosting (Netlify / Vercel / GitHub Pages).

## Users

Primary buyer: parents in Argentina looking for an English teacher for their children or teenagers. They usually arrive from a phone (WhatsApp, Instagram, Google search), compare a few options quickly, and decide based on trust: who the teacher is, whether their kid will enjoy it, and whether it actually helps at school.

Secondary reader: the teenager, who may look at the page with the parent.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for a private English teacher. Its single job: get the parent to write on WhatsApp to book a free trial class. Success = qualified WhatsApp conversations.

## Positioning

A real, certified teacher with institute experience (teaches at Legacy Institute) offering personal, one-to-one attention to kids and teens, online or in person. The difference against apps and big academies is the person: someone parents can trust and kids enjoy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page, one call to action (WhatsApp, free trial class). Minimal navigation, no clutter.
- Mobile-first: most visits come from phones.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first: parents buy the person, so she is the protagonist.
2. One action: every section leads to the same WhatsApp button.
3. Speak to parents, reassure about kids: progress at school, a class the kid enjoys, a safe and patient teacher.
4. Nothing unnecessary: no heavy nav, no filler sections.
